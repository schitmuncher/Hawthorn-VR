The forest levels of Journey to the Center of Hawkthorne, rebuilt as a 3D diorama for Meta Quest & WebXR.

Controls Quest — Left stick: move · up to climb · down to crouch
A/X jump · trigger or B: punch · down + A: drop through
Grip: grab & move the diorama (both grips: pull apart to resize) · right stick: resize
Y menu (point & pull trigger) · left stick click: recenter
Left-handed and one-controller layouts are in the menu.
Mixed reality — point at a wall to hang the game as a window, or at a table to stand it there; pull the trigger to place. Move it again from the menu.
Keyboard — ←→/AD move · ↑↓ climb/crouch · Space jump · X punch · Esc menu · +- zoom
Gamepad — stick/d-pad move · A jump · X/B/triggers punch · Start menu
Install — in the Quest browser, use the Install option (address bar or ⋯ menu) to add it to your library — it then runs offline and opens straight into VR.
